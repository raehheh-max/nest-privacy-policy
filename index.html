# NEST Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 14, 2026 ## 1. Introduction NEST is a school communication application designed to support communication among authorized users within Bulacan State University – Bustos Campus. This Privacy Policy explains how NEST collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when users access the application. ## 2. Information We Collect Depending on the features used, NEST may collect or process the following information: - **Account information:** Full name, email address, and account credentials or authentication identifiers. - **School information:** Student or institutional identification number and user role, where applicable. - **Communication data:** Messages, group conversations, and other content submitted through the application's communication features. - **Account status:** Information needed to manage account access, such as pending, active, or blocked status. NEST does not intentionally collect information unrelated to its educational communication purposes. ## 3. How We Use Information Personal information is used to: - Create and manage user accounts. - Authenticate users and control access to the application. - Identify users and assign appropriate account roles. - Enable school-related communication and group conversations. - Maintain application security and troubleshoot technical issues. - Administer and maintain the application's functionality. ## 4. Data Storage and Service Providers NEST uses Supabase for backend services, which may include authentication and database storage, depending on the application's configuration. Information processed through these services may be stored on or transmitted to Supabase infrastructure. Supabase processes information according to its applicable terms, privacy documentation, and service configuration. Supabase privacy information is available at: https://supabase.com/privacy ## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure NEST does not sell personal information. Personal information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application. Information may also be visible to authorized users or administrators when necessary for communication, account management, or application administration. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process. ## 6. Data Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures are intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Security depends on the application's implementation and configuration, including authentication, access controls, database permissions, and secure communications. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, manage accounts, meet legitimate administrative requirements, and comply with applicable obligations. Users who want to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal information may contact the application administrator using the contact details below. Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable retention requirements. ## 8. User Rights Subject to applicable law, users may request access to their personal information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion where appropriate. Users may also raise concerns about how their information is collected or processed through the contact provided below. ## 9. Children's Privacy NEST is intended for authorized members of the campus community. Where the application is used by minors, applicable privacy and child-protection requirements must be followed. ## 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same policy URL, with its effective date revised as appropriate. ## 11.
